How these results work

Diabetes, blood pressure and mental lifestyle estimates use age, gender, BMI, smoking status and today's sleep. Heart screening uses the same profile inputs without sleep.

Mental wellness answers update your daily score separately. Food, water, exercise and screen time contribute to daily wellness tracking, not these model percentages.

Changing an input may change an estimate without changing its risk category. Unchanged inputs produce the same estimate.
