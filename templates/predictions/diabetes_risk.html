How this prediction works

  1. Your answers are validated and mapped to the same 21 BRFSS features used by the trained pipeline.
  2. The local Scikit-learn model returns probabilities for no diabetes, prediabetes, and diabetes classes.
  3. The result and your submitted feature snapshot are saved to your account's assessment history.

This academic model is not clinically calibrated. Its class probability is not an individual medical probability, and the result is not a diagnosis.
